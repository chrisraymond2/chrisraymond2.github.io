Chris Raymond

Postdoctoral Fellow, Department of Mathematics, Universität Hamburg

I study vertex operator algebras (VOAs) and their representation theory as mathematical descriptions of two-dimensional conformal field theories.

I am a member of the Collaborative Research Center CRC 1624 “Higher Structures, Moduli Spaces and Integrability”, working with Sven Möller and Christoph Schweigert.

Research interests

Vertex operator algebras · representation theory · conformal field theory · tensor categories

  • VOAs and their modules related to logarithmic CFTs, such as fractional admissible-level affine VOAs and their associated W-algebras, particularly via (inverse) quantum hamiltonian reduction
  • VOAs arising as invariants of higher-dimensional quantum field theories, such as in the 3d/2d correspondences
  • Quantum deformations of VOAs related to affine Yangians
  • The relationship between VOAs and conformal nets as axiomatic descriptions of CFT
